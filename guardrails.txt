# One extended regex per line. Matched against each line ADDED on a factory branch, with
# diff's leading + still on it: anchor a pattern with ^\+, not ^ alone.
# Adapted from florianbuetow/ai-guardrails: no swallowed errors, no suppressed
# checks, no skipped tests. Edit to taste; blank lines and # comments are ignored.
# A pattern must not begin with a hash, or it is read as a comment: write [#] instead.

# swallowed shell failures; a || after a (, a comma in a ( not yet closed or a spaced =
# opens a closure instead, as in f(1, || true) and let f = || true, where an argument such
# as DESTDIR= has no space before its =, and one such as paste -sd, no ( open around it
(^|[^(,=[:space:]]|[^[:space:]]=|^[^(]*(\([^()]*\)[^(]*)*,)[[:space:]]*\|\|[[:space:]]*(true|:|exit 0|/bin/true)([^[:alnum:]_:]|$)
